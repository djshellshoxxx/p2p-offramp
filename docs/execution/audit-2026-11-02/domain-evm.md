## Verification and limits

Fresh verification consists of the 29-file semantic review, 50 focused passing T88 checks, baseline negative controls, both immutable receipt-manifest checks, snapshot/hash consistency and scoped whitespace validation. Historical suite/Foundry results are not counted as fresh. Root independent repair acceptance and final consolidated typecheck/package/build/contract validation remain pending; the T80 facade/schema work is concurrent with these receipts.

RPC consistency is not independent chain verification. Contract-wallet policy, issuer freezes, token honesty/proxy upgrades, chain inclusion/finality failure and seller withholding after manual fiat payment remain assumptions or economic risks. Canonical asset policy requires qualified plain exact-transfer assets. Legacy raw provider/calldata/log helpers and lifecycle queues partly rely on trusted caller admission, so this review makes no universal resource-bound claim.

Transport/messaging and crypto/storage were allocated to other reviewers. T80 implementation, upstream cryptographic/compiler/native code, live wallets/deployments and production qualification are outside this review. No commits, ledger changes, production activation or external-target testing were performed by this slice.
