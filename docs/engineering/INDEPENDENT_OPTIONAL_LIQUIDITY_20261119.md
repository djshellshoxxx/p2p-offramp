## Semantics and authority boundaries

The public facade exposes only `inspectVaultAccounting` and
`createLiquidityReference`. The latter returns `apply`, `observeQuote`, `view`
and `close`. Model and observation outputs explicitly remain diagnostic, with
`economicAuthority: false` and native reservation status `unavailable`. The only
port is a synchronous context reader. No wallet, network, signature, transaction,
escrow executor or payout adapter is present.

Actor, custody and outcome fields describe a simulation. They do not authenticate
an account or chain result. In particular, simulated refund and release commands
only move buckets for an already consumed simulated reservation. The package
README and `DECISIONS.json` explicitly preserve the missing native contracts and
do not promote these local commands into funded-trade authority.

Canonical decimal strings are checked against uint256/uint64 bounds and then
processed with integers. Conservation compares deposited principal with available,
reserved, escrowed, withdrawn and released buckets. Custody is tracked separately:
donations increase surplus without minting ledger credit. Safe withdrawal is
bounded by both available principal and custody above protected reserves. Checked
addition/subtraction rejects overflow or underflow before publishing state.

Reservations bind the exact maker, taker, asset, network, deployment, terms,
principal and local nonce. Fees are zero; positive principal is exact-fill only;
expiry is within the bounded 120-second window. Duplicate quote identifiers,
nonces and consumed trade identities remain unavailable. At most 128 quote/nonce
pairs are retained, including terminal tombstones; there is no replay-protection
eviction. Cancellation, expiry, consume and settlement are mutually constrained
state transitions. Failed transfers and reverted outcomes leave prior accounting
unchanged.

Unknown reserve, consume or withdrawal outcomes and reorg observations latch
reconciliation-required. Elapsed time, imported custody, cancellation and retry
commands cannot clear it. No automatic retry, timeout recovery or new production
reconciliation rule is supplied.

Exact own-data records reject unknown fields, accessors and hostile reflection.
Local errors are branded independently of arbitrary foreign exception values,
and diagnostics stay fixed. Mutating admission is acquired before input parsing
or context callbacks. Context is rechecked after parsing, and inactive scope,
malformed context or clock regression retires the model. Transitions compute on
copied state and commit without another foreign callback. Reentrant calls cannot
overlap a mutation, and closure during reflection prevents publication. `view`
is a frozen local snapshot, not a fresh chain assertion.
