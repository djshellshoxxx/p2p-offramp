Printable recovery checklist

  1. Preserve your encrypted backup and its passphrase separately. Keep private recovery references away from public hosting. A downloaded backup is not a tested restore; browser storage can be evicted.
  2. Identify the original escrow from independently authenticated records. Check its exact chain, genesis, address, protocol and runtime hash. Do not substitute a newly advertised deployment.
  3. Use an independently authenticated recovery package for that original deployment. The maintained package builder currently refuses an unset trust root. The instructions below describe its protocol; this portal does not supply an approved executable.
  4. Read fresh on-chain seller, buyer, asset, exact base-unit amount, state and deadline. Local labels, imported JSON and saved journal states are unverified references, not native chain evidence or proof of fiat receipt.
  5. The bound seller can release strictly before expiry. At or after expiry, anyone can trigger a refund to the bound seller. A fiat transfer does not force a seller release, and a proof does not guarantee irreversible payment.
  6. A deferred payout can require a withdrawal to its immutable beneficiary. There is no alternate recipient or automatic sweep. A rejecting recipient can still prevent delivery.
  7. If you use an external wallet, independently check network, sender, destination, calldata, zero value and fixed beneficiary; obtain current nonce, fees and adequate gas and perform a fresh simulation. Unsigned preparation does not authorize or guarantee inclusion.
  8. After a manual submission, retain its hash and verify the receipt, canonical inclusion, finalized policy and original escrow state. An uncertain broadcast is not proof of failure. Do not replay or automatically resubmit a stored request.

Public chain reads reveal metadata to the selected endpoint, including connection information. Private routing and encrypted local storage do not make public transactions anonymous. RPC responses are not independent consensus verification.
